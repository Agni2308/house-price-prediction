Objective
Compare Linear Regression and Random Forest for predicting house prices, then let anyone try the saved models on their own inputs.
Dataset
Bengaluru House Price Data from Kaggle: 13,320 listings, 11,767 after cleaning. The models use location, area type, total area, bedrooms, bathrooms and balconies.
Models

Linear Regression. Ordinary least squares on the preprocessed inputs. A simple, interpretable baseline.

Random Forest. An average of 100 decision trees (at least 3 samples per leaf, random_state 42). It can capture non-linear effects.

Technologies
Python, pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, Matplotlib, Flask, HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

How a prediction is made

  1. You enter the property details and choose a model.
  2. The API checks your input and applies the same preprocessing pipeline used in training.
  3. The saved model you chose predicts the price. Nothing is retrained.
  4. The estimated price is shown with a summary of your input.
